# Anhui Gujing Distillery Co., Ltd.

Anhui Gujing Distillery Co., Ltd. is a listed company whose main business is the production and sale of baijiu. Its registered and office addresses are in Gujing Town, Bozhou, Anhui. Public company disclosures list A shares under 000596 and B shares under 200596, both listed on the Shenzhen Stock Exchange in 1996.

## Company, brand and product

This record describes the **producer**. Gujing Gongjiu is a brand in the company's orbit; product lines and bottle specifications belong to other records:

- A brand record holds the name, brand relationships and public entry point.
- A product record holds the specific product, ABV, size and brand relationship.
- This page keeps company-level information and does not reproduce brand stories or tasting notes.
