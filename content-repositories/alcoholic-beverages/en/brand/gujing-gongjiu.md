# Gujing Gongjiu

Gujing Gongjiu is a strong-aroma baijiu brand from Bozhou, Anhui. Its “Nianfen Yuanjiang” line is widely known. The brand uses local water, mud-pit solid-state fermentation and continued mash recycling, and sells products at 40%–52% ABV.

## Style

The brand typically shows pit aroma, grain aroma and sweetness, with a soft entry. Different series and strengths differ enough that one tasting note cannot cover the whole range.

## About “Nianfen Yuanjiang”

“Nianfen Yuanjiang” is a product-line name, not proof that every drop carries the stated age. Check the label and the producer's public information for the actual age claim and blend.

## Boundary

This page describes the brand and category, not a specific batch. Buy through official channels and check the anti-counterfeit label, production batch and product information.
