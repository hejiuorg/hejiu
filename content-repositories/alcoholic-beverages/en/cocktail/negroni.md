# Negroni

The Negroni is a classic cocktail made with gin, a bitter aperitif and sweet vermouth. The International Bartenders Association (IBA) published recipe uses 30 ml of each, stirred and strained into a rocks glass over ice.

## Identity and versions

This page records the cocktail's **identity**: its name, basic composition, style and origin story. The executable quantities, steps and equipment live in a [recipe version](ref:recipe). One cocktail identity may have several sourced versions; no single unsourced version should overwrite the others.

## How to write the origin story

A common account says that in early twentieth-century Florence, Count Camillo Negroni asked a bartender to replace the soda water in an Americano with gin. The story is widely repeated in cocktail literature, but finer dates and biographical details should still be presented as a traditional account rather than proven archival fact.

## How it is served

The IBA recipe strains the drink into a [rocks glass](ref:vessel) over ice and garnishes with orange peel or an orange slice. Smell the citrus peel first, then sip slowly; temperature and dilution change as the ice melts.
