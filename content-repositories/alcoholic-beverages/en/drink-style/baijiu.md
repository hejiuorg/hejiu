# Baijiu

Baijiu is a family of traditional Chinese distilled spirits. In GB/T 15109-2021, Terminology for the baijiu industry, it is generally defined as a grain-based spirit produced with saccharifying and fermenting agents such as daqu, xiaoqu, fuqu or yeast, followed by cooking, saccharification, fermentation, distillation, maturation and blending.

## Start with the definition, then the style

“Baijiu” describes a type of spirit and a production tradition. A bottle on a shelf still needs further distinctions: style, brand, producer and product. One brand may cover several products, and one style can have many expressions.

Aroma family is a common way to organise styles, such as [strong aroma](ref:strong-aroma). It is a useful first direction, not a quality ranking, and it does not replace information about region, ingredients, process or vintage.

## Questions worth asking about a glass

- Which style is this, and what source supports that classification?
- Which grains, qu type and fermentation vessel are used?
- Who is the producer, and which brand and product are in front of you?
- Where do the ABV, bottle size and vintage information come from?
- Is a statement a standard definition, producer disclosure, or a taster's opinion?
