# Strong-aroma baijiu

Strong-aroma baijiu is one style within [baijiu](ref:baijiu). GB/T 10781.1-2021, Quality requirements for baijiu — Part 1: Strong-aroma baijiu, sets out terminology, requirements and testing rules for this product category.

## Process characteristics

Strong-aroma production commonly uses mud pits for solid-state fermentation, continuous grain topping across multiple rounds, and mixed steaming during distillation. Regions, producers and products vary in their qu, pit management, fermentation length and blending.

These process notes are not an endorsement of any particular bottle. For a specific product, return to its producer, region, ingredients, ABV and declared standard.

## How to use flavour language

Phrases such as “rich pit aroma” can be a useful shorthand for the style. They should not become a fixed score for every product. A better record names the taster, the product, the serving temperature and the glass, and keeps subjective impressions separate from verifiable facts.
