# China

This is the country node of the region hierarchy. It organises provinces, producers and places; it is not a drink category and does not imply that every drink comes from one region.

## Place in the knowledge graph

- Province records use this page as their parent.
- Styles, brands and products point to specific regions through their own `from-region` relations.
- A place keeps its own address and opening information. Those details are not copied into the region page.

## References

Administrative codes follow GB/T 2260-2007; province and city entries follow public administrative division information.
