# Anhui

Anhui is a province in eastern China. The Yangtze and Huai rivers cross it from west to east. This page is an organising node for regions; it does not treat “Anhui” as a drink style.

## Relationship to drink content

- City nodes such as Bozhou carry more specific place and producer relationships.
- Producers, brands and products point to a city or province through region relations.
- A style page does not claim that every local product tastes the same simply because it comes from the province.
