# Absolut Vodka

Absolut Vodka is made from Swedish winter wheat in Åhus and continuously distilled. It is commonly 40% ABV and known for a clean, soft texture and its distinctive bottle.

## Style

Vodka flavour differences come mainly from raw material, distillation and filtration; not every vodka is completely neutral. Absolut is typically clean with a light cereal sweetness and works well as a cocktail base.

## Drinking

Serve well chilled, or use it in a Moscow Mule, Bloody Mary or vodka martini. Because the spirit is neutral, it provides structure rather than a dominant flavour, but its strength still affects the balance of a drink.

## Boundary

This page describes the brand and category, not a specific product. Check the label and official information for raw material, distillation and additives.
