# Erguotou

Erguotou is a traditional Beijing baijiu process and product category. The name refers to collecting the spirit that condenses from the second distillation pot. It is usually made from sorghum with fuqu or daqu, and common strengths range from 43% to 56% ABV. The style is direct and light-aroma.

## Style

Erguotou tends to have a punchy entry, direct aroma and a clean finish; prices vary widely. It is not a single brand: Red Star, Niulanshan and other producers all make erguotou, with different raw materials, starters and blends.

## Drinking

Traditionally served neat in a small cup, it is also used in cocktails and infused fruit liquors. Because it is strong, sip it slowly. With Beijing snacks, salt and fat change the perception of the alcohol.

## Boundary

This page describes the process category, not a specific brand. Check the label and producer information for ingredients, strength and production standard.
