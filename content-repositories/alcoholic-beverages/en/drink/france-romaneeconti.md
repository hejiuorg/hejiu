# Romanée-Conti

Romanée-Conti is a grand cru vineyard in Vosne-Romanée, Burgundy, owned by Domaine de la Romanée-Conti (DRC). It is planted entirely to Pinot Noir. Production is very small, and the wine is known for aromatic complexity and long ageing potential.

## Style

Young Romanée-Conti often shows red berries, rose, spice and minerals, with fine tannins; with age it develops mushroom, leaf and leather notes. Its reputation also reflects tiny quantities and long-term scarcity, not only the wine in the glass.

## Drinking

Serve at about 15–17°C. Old bottles usually need little decanting, as too much air can let the aroma fade. Use a Burgundy glass and pour small amounts.

## Boundary

“DRC” is often used loosely for all wines from the domaine, which also makes other grand crus. Check the label, producer information and expert authentication for the specific wine, vintage and authenticity.
