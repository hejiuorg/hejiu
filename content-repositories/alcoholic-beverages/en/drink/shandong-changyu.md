# Changyu Wine

Changyu is a wine brand from Yantai, Shandong. The company was founded in 1892 and is one of China's earliest modern wineries. Its range includes red and white wines and brandy, with varieties such as Cabernet Gernischt, Cabernet Sauvignon and Chardonnay.

## Style

The range runs from everyday wines to estate bottlings. Yantai's maritime climate affects grape ripeness, and the series, vintage and variety matter more than the brand name alone.

## Cabernet Gernischt

Cabernet Gernischt is one of China's signature grape varieties. Its relationship to Carmenère has been discussed in ampelographic research; the label and the producer's testing are the reliable source for the variety.

## Drinking

Serve reds with red meat and strongly flavoured Chinese dishes, and whites with seafood and lighter dishes. Red wines are usually served at 16–18°C and whites at 8–12°C. Drink opened bottles soon.

## Boundary

This page describes the brand and category, not a specific product. Check the label and official information for variety, vintage and origin.
