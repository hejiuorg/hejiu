# Château Lafite Rothschild

Château Lafite Rothschild is in Pauillac, Bordeaux, and was ranked a first growth in the 1855 classification. The grand vin is based mainly on Cabernet Sauvignon with Merlot, Cabernet Franc and Petit Verdot, and typically has a firm structure and long ageing potential.

## Style

Young Lafite often shows blackcurrant, cedar, graphite and tobacco, with fine tannins. With age it develops leather, mushroom and cedar complexity. Vintages vary greatly; one year cannot represent the estate.

## Drinking

Serve at about 16–18°C. Young bottles can be decanted for one to two hours; old bottles need less air and careful handling of sediment. It pairs with red meat, game and hard cheese.

## Boundary

“Lafite” is often used loosely in Chinese. The grand vin is labelled Château Lafite Rothschild; the second wine and other group products are different wines. Check the label and official channels for vintage, batch and authenticity.
