# Pilsner Urquell

Pilsner Urquell is brewed in Plzeň, Czechia, and was first brewed in 1842. It is one of the prototypes of pale lager, using local soft water, Saaz hops, triple decoction and open fermentation. It is commonly about 4.4% ABV.

## Style

It typically shows malt sweetness, herbal and spicy Saaz hop aroma, a clear but balanced bitterness and a clean finish. It is a pale lager, not a heavy or highly bitter IPA.

## Drinking

Serve at about 6–8°C in a clean glass. Czech serving styles create different foam thicknesses, which affect aroma and texture; at home, simple careful pouring is enough.

## Boundary

“Pilsner” has become a style name, but only specific products have the original designation. Check the label and official information for ingredients, original gravity and strength.
