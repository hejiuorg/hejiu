# Bacardí Carta Blanca

Bacardí Carta Blanca is a white rum made from molasses, fermented, distilled and filtered through charcoal. It is commonly 40% ABV. The brand began in Santiago de Cuba in 1862 and is now headquartered in Bermuda.

## Style

White rum is usually light and clean, with sugarcane sweetness and a hint of vanilla. Charcoal filtration removes colour and some heavier flavours, making it a versatile cocktail base.

## Drinking

Use it in a Mojito, Daiquiri or Cuba Libre. Balance the lime, sugar and ice dilution; too much syrup masks the rum. It can be served over ice, but the flavour is direct.
