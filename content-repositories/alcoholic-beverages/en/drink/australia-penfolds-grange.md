# Penfolds Grange

Penfolds Grange is the flagship wine of Penfolds in South Australia. Max Schubert made the first vintage in 1951. It is based mainly on Shiraz, often with a small proportion of Cabernet Sauvignon, and is aged in American oak.

## Style

Grange is typically full-bodied, with blackberry, black pepper, mocha and cedar notes, firm tannins and a long ageing potential. It is a multi-region blend rather than a single-vineyard wine; the vintage on the label is the grape harvest year.

## Drinking

Serve at about 16–18°C. Young vintages can be decanted for one to two hours; older bottles need care with sediment. It pairs with roast beef, lamb and hard cheese.

## Boundary

This page describes the brand and category, not a specific vintage. Scarcity and price do not guarantee that every vintage is equally strong; check the label and official information.
