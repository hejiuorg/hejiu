# Chartreuse

Chartreuse is a herbal liqueur made by the Carthusian monks in Voiron, France. The recipe uses 130 plants; green Chartreuse is 55% ABV and yellow Chartreuse is 40% ABV.

## Style

Green Chartreuse is stronger and more intense, with herbal, anise and mint notes; yellow is sweeter and softer. It is a liqueur, not a medicine, and the monastic recipe is part of the brand's history rather than public information.

## Drinking

Serve neat, over ice, or in classics such as the Last Word and Champs-Élysées. Its intensity means small amounts are usually enough in a cocktail.

## Boundary

This page describes the brand and category, not a specific batch. Production location and the full recipe are proprietary; check official information for strength and labelling.
