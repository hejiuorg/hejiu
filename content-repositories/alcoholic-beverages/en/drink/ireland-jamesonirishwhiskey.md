# Jameson Irish Whiskey

Jameson is an Irish blended whiskey produced at Midleton and triple-distilled. It is commonly 40% ABV, with grain sweetness, green apple and vanilla notes and a smooth texture.

## Style

Triple distillation gives a lighter body and softer alcohol; blending balances grain whiskey with pot still whiskey. It is a good introduction to Irish whiskey, but it does not represent every Irish style.

## Drinking

Serve neat, over ice or with soda; it is also used in Irish coffee. Too much water can flatten the grain sweetness.

## Boundary

This page describes the brand and category, not a specific batch. Irish whiskey must be produced and aged in the protected region and aged at least three years; check the label for the exact ageing and cask information.
