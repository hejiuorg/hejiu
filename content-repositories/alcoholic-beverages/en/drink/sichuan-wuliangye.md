# Wuliangye

Wuliangye is produced in Yibin, Sichuan, from sorghum, rice, glutinous rice, wheat and corn. It is a strong-aroma baijiu. Long-used mud pits, continued mash recycling and mixed steaming and boiling are central to its production. A common strength is 52% ABV.

## Style

The aroma is led by pit, grain and sweet notes, with a mellow entry, a full body and a clean finish. Ethyl hexanoate is associated with strong-aroma baijiu, but pit age, vintage and blending create noticeable differences.

## Drinking

Serve neat in a small cup. With spicy Sichuan food, the heat can amplify the alcohol; take smaller sips. Ice is not traditional, but it lowers the alcohol impact and changes the aroma.

## Boundary

This page describes the category and production, not a specific vintage or batch. Buy through official channels and check the label, anti-counterfeit mark and production information.
