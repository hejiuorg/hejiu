# Yanghe

Yanghe is produced in Suqian, Jiangsu, and belongs to the strong-aroma family. Its modern identity is built around a “soft and mellow” style, with the Haitian, Tianzhi and Mengzhi Blue series covering strengths from about 38% to 52% ABV.

## Style

“Soft and mellow” is a flavour description, not a legal aroma category. The base remains strong-aroma solid-state fermentation, but Yanghe emphasises a gentle entry, noticeable sweetness and low alcohol bite. Different strengths and series vary considerably.

## Drinking

Choose the serving style by strength: lower-strength products can be served neat or over ice, while higher-strength products suit small sips. With light Huaiyang dishes, sweetness and umami can change how the alcohol is perceived.

## Boundary

This page describes the brand and category, not a specific product. Check the label and official information for base-spirit age, blending and strength.
