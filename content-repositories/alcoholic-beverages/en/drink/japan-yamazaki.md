# Yamazaki Single Malt

Yamazaki is a malt whisky distillery in Yamazaki, Kyoto, established by Suntory in 1923. It is one of the starting points of the Japanese whisky industry. Yamazaki single malts often use mizunara and sherry casks and are commonly 43% ABV.

## Style

Yamazaki is typically delicate and balanced, with floral and fruit notes, honey and a sandalwood-like woodiness. Mizunara casks bring incense, coconut and spice; age and cask selection create wide variation.

## Drinking

Serve neat or with a few drops of water in a tulip glass. Ice lowers the aroma; a highball is a common everyday serve in Japan but dilutes the detail.

## Boundary

This page describes the brand and category, not a specific batch. Japanese whisky labelling rules have tightened in recent years; check the label and industry standard for whether a product qualifies.
