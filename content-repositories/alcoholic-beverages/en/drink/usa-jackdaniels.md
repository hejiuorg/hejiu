# Jack Daniel's Old No. 7

Jack Daniel's Old No. 7 is a Tennessee whiskey made in Lynchburg, Tennessee. New distillate is filtered through maple charcoal before ageing in new charred oak barrels, the Lincoln County Process. It is commonly 40% ABV.

## Style

Charcoal filtration softens the entry, while vanilla, caramel, banana and oak notes remain prominent. It uses new charred oak like bourbon; Tennessee whiskey is a separate category, with charcoal filtration as its signature step.

## Drinking

Serve neat, over ice, or with cola. A highball brings out vanilla and wood; ice reduces sweetness and alcohol bite.

This page describes the brand and category, not a specific batch. Check the label and official information for ageing, cask type and bottling strength.
