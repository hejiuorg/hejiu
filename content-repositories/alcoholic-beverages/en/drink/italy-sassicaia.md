# Sassicaia

Sassicaia is a wine from Bolgheri in Tuscany, made by Tenuta San Guido and based on Cabernet Sauvignon and Cabernet Franc. It is one of the best-known “Super Tuscans” and has its own DOC.

## Style

It typically shows blackcurrant, cedar, Mediterranean scrub and mineral notes, with medium to full body and fine tannins. It does not rely on Sangiovese, so it is different from traditional Chianti.

## Drinking

Serve at about 16–18°C; young vintages can be decanted for about an hour. It pairs with Tuscan steak, roast meat and hard cheese.

## Boundary

“Super Tuscan” is a market term, not an official classification. Check the label and estate information for the variety blend, vintage and appellation rules.
