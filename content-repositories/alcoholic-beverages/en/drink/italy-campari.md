# Campari

Campari is an Italian bitter aperitif created by Gaspare Campari in Milan in 1860. It is infused with herbs, roots and citrus and usually 20.5%–25% ABV, with a balance of bitterness and sweetness.

## Style

Campari has a pronounced bitter edge, orange peel and herbal notes, and moderate sweetness. It is an aromatised drink, not a gin; it provides the red bitter element in a Negroni. Recipes and strengths vary slightly by market.

## Drinking

Serve over ice with soda and orange, or use it in a Negroni, Americano or Campari Spritz. For a first taste, Campari soda lowers the alcohol and bitterness.
