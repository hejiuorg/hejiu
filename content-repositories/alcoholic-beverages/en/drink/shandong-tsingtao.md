# Tsingtao Beer

Tsingtao Beer is produced in Qingdao, Shandong, and is one of China's oldest beer brands, founded in 1903 by German and British merchants. Its main products are bottom-fermented lagers, commonly around 4% ABV.

## Style

Tsingtao is typically clean and refreshing, with moderate bitterness, light malt sweetness and a mild hop aroma. The range includes classic, draft, wheat and dark beers, and these differ enough that one description cannot cover them all.

## Drinking

Serve well chilled in a clean glass so the foam and aroma can develop. It suits seafood, grilled food and neutral dishes. Repeated warming and long storage accelerate oxidation.

This page describes the brand and category, not a specific product. Check the label and official information for ingredients, original gravity and strength.
