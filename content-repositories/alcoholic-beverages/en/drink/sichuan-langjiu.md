# Langjiu

Langjiu is produced in Erlang, Gulin, Sichuan, on the Chishui River. The company makes both sauce-aroma and strong-aroma baijiu; its sauce-aroma products, including Qinghua Lang, are widely known. A common strength is 53% ABV.

## Style

Sauce-aroma products typically show soy-like, toasted and floral notes with a full body; strong-aroma products emphasise pit and grain aroma. The river-valley climate and water are part of the production context, but different plants and product lines are not identical.

## Drinking

Sauce-aroma products suit neat sipping in small cups; strong-aroma products can be served neat or with food. Avoid rapid drinking and keep opened bottles away from heat and sunlight.

## Boundary

This page describes the brand and category, not a specific product. Check the label and official information for aroma type, vintage and strength.
