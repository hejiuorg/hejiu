# Fenjiu

Fenjiu is produced in Xinghuacun, Fenyang, Shanxi, and is a leading light-aroma baijiu. It uses sorghum with a barley and pea qu and a “clean steam, second clear” process. A common strength is 53% ABV.

## Style

Light-aroma baijiu emphasises cleanliness and a crisp finish. Ethyl acetate leads the aroma, with grain and light fruit notes. The entry is mellow and the finish is dry. It is deliberately different from the pit aroma of strong-aroma baijiu and the toasted notes of sauce aroma.

## Drinking

Serve neat in a small cup. Some drinkers warm it gently, but overheating damages the aroma. Judge it on its own terms rather than by the standards of another aroma family.
