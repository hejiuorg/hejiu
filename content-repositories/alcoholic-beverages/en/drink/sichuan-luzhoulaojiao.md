# Luzhou Laojiao

Luzhou Laojiao is produced in Luzhou, Sichuan, and is a leading strong-aroma baijiu. Its old mud pits have been used for long periods, and the combination of continued mash recycling and mixed steaming and boiling produces a pronounced pit aroma. A common strength is 52% ABV.

## Style

Pit aroma, grain aroma and ethyl hexanoate dominate, with a sweet, mellow entry, a full body and a clean finish. The microbial community in old pits is important, but “older pit, better liquor” remains a rule of thumb, not a substitute for tasting.

## Drinking

Serve neat in a small cup with strongly flavoured Chinese dishes. Keep the bottle away from heat and sunlight after opening.
