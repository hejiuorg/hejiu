# Dom Pérignon

Dom Pérignon is a vintage Champagne produced by Moët & Chandon only in declared years. It is a blend of Chardonnay and Pinot Noir, commonly 12.5% ABV.

## Style

It emphasises acidity, minerality and the toasted, bready notes that develop during lees ageing. Each vintage has its own blend, disgorgement date and time on the lees; “vintage Champagne” means the wine comes mainly from the stated harvest year.

## Drinking

Chill to about 8–10°C and use a tulip or white-wine glass to gather aroma. Open carefully to avoid a violent release of foam; give the wine a moment after pouring for the bubbles and aroma to settle.

## Boundary

This page describes the brand and category, not a specific vintage. Dosage, vintage and disgorgement details are on the label; Dom Pérignon is not a non-vintage Champagne.
