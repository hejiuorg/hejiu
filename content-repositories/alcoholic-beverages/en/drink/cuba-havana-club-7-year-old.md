# Havana Club 7

Havana Club 7 is a Cuban rum brand made from molasses-derived spirit and blended and aged in Cuba. It is commonly 40% ABV, with vanilla, caramel, tobacco and tropical-fruit notes.

## Style

It is a rounded aged rum, suited to sipping or mixing. The “7” in the name is an age statement in the product name; the exact age structure of the blend is decided by the producer and is not the same as a single 7-year-old rum.

## Drinking

Serve neat with a large ice cube, or use it in Cuban-style cocktails. Cola masks much of the detail; a Daiquiri or Old Fashioned variation shows more of the rum.

## Boundary

This page describes the brand and category, not a specific batch. Check the label and official information for ageing, sweetness and origin.
