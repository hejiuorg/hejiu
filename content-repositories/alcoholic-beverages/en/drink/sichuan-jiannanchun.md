# Jiannanchun

Jiannanchun is produced in Mianzhu, Sichuan, and belongs to the strong-aroma family. It uses sorghum, rice, glutinous rice, wheat and corn, with mud-pit solid-state fermentation and continued mash recycling. A common strength is 52% ABV.

## Style

It is often described as full, mellow and slightly brisk, with pit and grain aroma in balance, a sweet entry and a clean finish. Its climate, pits and blending differ from those of southern Sichuan strong-aroma producers, so the styles are not identical.

## Drinking

Serve neat in a small cup, or with strongly flavoured food. For a first taste, pour a small amount, smell it, then sip slowly; avoid drinking quickly on an empty stomach.
