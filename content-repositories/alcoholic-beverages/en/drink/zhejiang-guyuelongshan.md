# Guyuelongshan Huadiao

Guyuelongshan is a Shaoxing huangjiu brand from Zhejiang. It makes huadiao, jiafan and related Shaoxing wines from glutinous rice, wheat qu and Jian Lake water, followed by pressing, pasteurisation and ageing. Alcohol is usually 15%–18% ABV.

## Style

Shaoxing huangjiu typically shows rice, wheat-qu and aged notes, with a texture ranging from semi-dry to semi-sweet. Huadiao is a traditional name, not a fixed sweetness level. Jiafan is usually semi-dry, shanniang is sweeter, and the label is the reliable guide to sugar content.

## Drinking

Serve warm at about 35–45°C, at room temperature, or chilled. Warmth opens the aroma and softens the alcohol, but overheating damages the flavour. It pairs well with hairy crab, braised pork and soy-braised duck.

## Boundary

This page describes the brand and category, not a specific vintage. Check the label and official information for sugar content, age and whether caramel colour is added.
