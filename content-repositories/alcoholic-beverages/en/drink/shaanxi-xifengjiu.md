# Xifengjiu

Xifengjiu is produced in Fengxiang, Shaanxi, and is the leading phoenix-aroma baijiu. It uses sorghum with a barley, wheat and pea qu, ferments in earthen pits and stores the spirit in traditional wine-sea containers. A common strength is 55% ABV.

## Style

Phoenix aroma sits between light and strong aroma, often showing a mix of ethyl hexanoate and ethyl acetate, a firm entry and a long finish. The traditional “wine sea” storage vessel, made from woven willow and lined with blood and egg white, contributes to the style.

## Drinking

Serve neat in a small cup with northwestern Chinese meat and wheat dishes. The high strength rewards small sips; the firm acidity and alcohol can seem sharp at first.
