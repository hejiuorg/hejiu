# Hennessy XO

Hennessy XO is a Cognac blended from more than 100 eaux-de-vie. It is commonly 40% ABV. XO is a legal minimum ageing tier in Cognac, not a single vintage.

## Style

The blend typically shows dried fruit, cocoa, spice and wood, with a full body and a long finish. The exact blend changes between batches; the goal is consistency of house style rather than the character of one year.

## Drinking

Serve neat in a tulip glass at room temperature, or let the glass warm in your hand. Ice lowers the aroma but can suit a high-strength or younger batch. It also works in a Sidecar.

## Boundary

This page describes the brand and category, not a specific batch. Cognac age grades have legal minimums; check the bottle and official information for the exact product.
