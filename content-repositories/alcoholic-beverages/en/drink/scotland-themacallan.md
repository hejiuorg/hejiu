# The Macallan 18 Sherry Oak

The Macallan is a Speyside single malt brand known for sherry-seasoned oak maturation. The Macallan 18 Sherry Oak is commonly 43% ABV, with dried fruit, spice and dark chocolate notes.

## Style

Sherry casks bring raisin, fig, orange peel and wood spice, with a full body. Different releases, batches and cask types vary; “18 years” is the minimum age of the liquid, not the age of a single cask.

## Drinking

Serve neat to experience the full aroma, or add a little room-temperature water to soften the alcohol and open the dried-fruit and spice notes. Ice cools and dilutes, which can suit a stronger or heavier batch.

## Boundary

This page describes the brand and category, not a specific batch. Check the bottle and official information for cask type, bottling strength and release details.
