# Guinness Draught

Guinness Draught is a dry stout brewed at St James’s Gate in Dublin. It is commonly about 4.2% ABV. Roasted barley gives its dark colour and coffee-like bitterness, and the nitrogenated version has a particularly smooth, creamy foam.

## Style

Stout is not a synonym for “dark beer”, and dark colour does not mean a heavy body. Guinness Draught is medium-light in body; its bitterness comes from roasted barley, and nitrogen creates fine bubbles and a soft texture.

## Drinking

Serve at about 6–8°C in a pint glass. The nitrogen version settles visibly after pouring. It pairs with oysters, stews and Irish bread.

## Boundary

This page describes the brand and category, not a specific product. Canned, bottled and draught versions use different packaging and gas methods; check the label and official information.
