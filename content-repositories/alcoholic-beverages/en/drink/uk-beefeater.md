# Beefeater London Dry Gin

Beefeater is a London dry gin founded by James Burrough in 1863. It is distilled with juniper, citrus peel, angelica and other botanicals and is commonly 40% ABV; some versions are 47%.

## Style

Beefeater typically shows prominent juniper, fresh citrus and dry spice. London dry is a production category, not a place of origin: botanicals must be added during distillation, and the product cannot rely on added flavouring for its main character.

## Drinking

Use it in a martini, gin and tonic or Negroni. Tonic's sweetness and bitterness can amplify or mask botanical detail, and the amount of ice and lime changes the balance.

## Boundary

This page describes the brand and category, not a specific batch. Check the label and official information for botanicals, distillation and strength.
