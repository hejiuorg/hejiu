- [Penfolds Grange](australia-penfolds-grange.md) — The flagship wine of Penfolds, first made by Max Schubert in 1951 and based mainly on Shiraz.
- [Erguotou](beijing-erguotou.md) — A traditional Beijing baijiu style and product category, named for taking the spirit from the second distillation pot.
- [Bacardí Carta Blanca](cuba-bacardi-carta-blanca.md) — A white rum made from molasses, charcoal-filtered and commonly bottled at 40% ABV.
- [Havana Club 7](cuba-havana-club-7-year-old.md) — A Cuban aged rum blend, commonly 40% ABV, with vanilla, caramel and tobacco notes.
- [Pilsner Urquell](czech-pilsnerurquell.md) — A pale lager from Plzeň, Czechia, first brewed in 1842 and commonly around 4.4% ABV.
- [Chartreuse](france-chartreuse.md) — A herbal liqueur made by the Carthusian monks in Voiron, France; green is 55% ABV and yellow 40%.
- [Château Lafite Rothschild](france-chateaulafiterothschild.md) — A first-growth Pauillac estate in Bordeaux, known for Cabernet Sauvignon-based blends with long ageing potential.
- [Dom Pérignon](france-domperignon.md) — A vintage Champagne from Moët & Chandon, produced only in declared years and commonly 12.5% ABV.
- [Hennessy XO](france-hennessyxo.md) — A Cognac blended from more than 100 eaux-de-vie, commonly 40% ABV; XO is a minimum ageing tier.
- [Romanée-Conti](france-romaneeconti.md) — A Burgundy grand cru in Vosne-Romanée owned by Domaine de la Romanée-Conti, planted entirely to Pinot Noir.
- [Moutai](guizhou-moutai.md) — A sauce-aroma baijiu from Maotai, Guizhou, made from sorghum and wheat qu and commonly bottled at 53% ABV.
- [Jiugui](hunan-jiugui.md) — A “fuyu-aroma” baijiu from Jishou, Hunan, combining strong-, light- and sauce-aroma production features.
- [Guinness Draught](ireland-guinnessdraughtstout.md) — An Irish dry stout from St James’s Gate in Dublin, commonly about 4.2% ABV.
- [Jameson Irish Whiskey](ireland-jamesonirishwhiskey.md) — An Irish blended whiskey from Midleton, triple-distilled and commonly 40% ABV.
- [Campari](italy-campari.md) — An Italian bitter aperitif created in Milan in 1860, usually 20.5–25% ABV.
- [Sassicaia](italy-sassicaia.md) — A Bolgheri wine from Tenuta San Guido, based on Cabernet Sauvignon and Cabernet Franc.
- [Yamazaki Single Malt](japan-yamazaki.md) — A Japanese single malt from Suntory’s Yamazaki distillery, which began production in 1923.
- [Yanghe](jiangsu-yanghe.md) — A strong-aroma baijiu brand from Suqian, Jiangsu, known for a “soft and mellow” style and the Blue Classic series.
- [The Macallan 18 Sherry Oak](scotland-themacallan.md) — A Speyside single malt aged in sherry-seasoned oak and commonly bottled at 43% ABV.
- [Xifengjiu](shaanxi-xifengjiu.md) — A phoenix-aroma baijiu from Fengxiang, Shaanxi, made with sorghum and a barley, wheat and pea qu.
- [Changyu Wine](shandong-changyu.md) — A wine brand from Yantai, Shandong; Changyu was founded in 1892 and makes red, white and brandy products.
- [Tsingtao Beer](shandong-tsingtao.md) — A lager brand from Qingdao, Shandong, founded in 1903 and commonly around 4% ABV.
- [Fenjiu](shanxi-fenjiu.md) — A light-aroma baijiu from Xinghuacun, Fenyang, Shanxi, made with sorghum and a barley-pea qu.
- [Jiannanchun](sichuan-jiannanchun.md) — A strong-aroma baijiu from Mianzhu, Sichuan, made from five grains and fermented in mud pits.
- [Langjiu](sichuan-langjiu.md) — A baijiu producer based in Erlang, Gulin, Sichuan, making both sauce-aroma and strong-aroma products.
- [Luzhou Laojiao](sichuan-luzhoulaojiao.md) — A strong-aroma baijiu from Luzhou, Sichuan, known for long-used mud pits and continued mash fermentation.
- [Wuliangye](sichuan-wuliangye.md) — A strong-aroma baijiu from Yibin, Sichuan, made from five grains and commonly bottled at 52% ABV.
- [Absolut Vodka](sweden-absolutvodka.md) — A Swedish vodka made from winter wheat and continuously distilled, commonly 40% ABV.
- [Beefeater London Dry Gin](uk-beefeater.md) — A London dry gin founded in 1863, distilled with juniper and citrus botanicals and commonly 40% ABV.
- [Jack Daniel's Old No. 7](usa-jackdaniels.md) — A Tennessee whiskey made with the Lincoln County Process and commonly 40% ABV.
- [Guyuelongshan Huadiao](zhejiang-guyuelongshan.md) — A Shaoxing huangjiu brand from Zhejiang, brewed from glutinous rice, wheat qu and Jian Lake water.
