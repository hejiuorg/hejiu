# Moutai

Moutai is produced in Maotai, Renhuai, Guizhou, and is a leading example of sauce-aroma baijiu. It uses sorghum and a high-temperature wheat qu, with repeated cooking, fermentation and distillation; the base spirit is aged and blended. The common Feitian Moutai is 53% ABV.

## Style

Sauce-aroma baijiu is often described as combining soy-like, toasted, floral and cooked-fruit notes with a full body and a long finish. The river-valley climate and long-established microbial environment are part of its production context; “you cannot make Moutai outside Maotai” is an industry saying, not a strict scientific claim.

## Drinking

Serve neat in a small cup. Smell first, then sip slowly and notice the alcohol, texture and returning sweetness. Ice or a large amount of water changes the aroma; if adding water, do it drop by drop.
