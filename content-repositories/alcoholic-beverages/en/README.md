## Drink styles

- [Aperitif](drink-style/aperitif.md) — A pre-meal drink category defined by occasion and a dry or bitter profile, not by one base ingredient.
- [Medicinal liquor](drink-style/baijiu-medicinal.md) — A Chinese infused liquor made with a distilled or yellow-wine base and food-medicine ingredients; it is food, not medicine.
- [Baijiu](drink-style/baijiu.md) — A traditional Chinese distilled spirit made mainly from grains and fermented with qu. Aroma family is one useful lens, not the only one.
- [Beer](drink-style/beer.md) — A fermented cereal drink, usually hopped, ranging from clean lagers to aromatic ales, stouts and sour beers.
- [Brandy](drink-style/brandy.md) — A spirit distilled from wine or other fruit wine, including Cognac, Armagnac, Calvados and grape-pomace brandies.
- [Fruit wine](drink-style/fruit-wine.md) — A fermented drink made from fruit juice, distinct from fruit liqueur and fruit brandy.
- [Gin](drink-style/gin.md) — A juniper-led spirit, with London dry, distilled gin and compound gin as distinct production categories.
- [Liqueur](drink-style/liqueur.md) — A sweetened spirit flavoured with fruit, herbs, nuts, cream or coffee; bitters are a separate, less sweet category.
- [Rum](drink-style/rum.md) — A spirit distilled from sugarcane juice or molasses, with white, aged, dark and agricole styles.
- [Sake](drink-style/sake.md) — A Japanese fermented rice drink made with rice, water, koji and yeast, classified by polishing and brewing method.
- [Soju](drink-style/soju.md) — A Korean spirit ranging from traditional distilled soju to modern diluted soju, distinct from Japanese shochu.
- [Strong-aroma baijiu](drink-style/strong-aroma-baijiu.md) — A baijiu style associated with mud-pit solid-state fermentation, continuous grain topping and mixed steaming. Style is not a quality score.
- [Tequila](drink-style/tequila.md) — A Mexican spirit distilled from blue Weber agave, with 100% agave and mixto expressions and several age tiers.
- [Vodka](drink-style/vodka.md) — A neutral spirit made from grain, potatoes, molasses or other materials, usually 37.5% ABV or higher.
- [Whisky](drink-style/whisky.md) — A grain spirit aged in oak, with Scottish, Irish, American and Japanese traditions and strict labelling rules.
- [Wine](drink-style/wine.md) — Fermented grape juice, ranging from still red, white and rosé to sparkling, sweet and fortified wines.
- [Yellow wine](drink-style/yellow-wine.md) — A Chinese fermented grain wine, usually rice-based; Shaoxing is its best-known protected origin.
