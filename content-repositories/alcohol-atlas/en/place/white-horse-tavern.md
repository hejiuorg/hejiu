# White Horse Tavern

The White Horse Tavern is in Greenwich Village and opened in 1880. It is known for the poet Dylan Thomas, who was a regular, and has long been a meeting place for writers, journalists and local residents.

## Style

The bar has a wooden counter, old photographs and the feel of a neighbourhood pub. It serves beer, spirits and simple bar food; tourists and locals mix, and weekends are crowded.

## Drinking

Order a beer or whiskey and sit at the bar. Dylan Thomas's story is historical context; it should not romanticise heavy drinking.

Opening hours, menus and prices are set by official channels. Accounts of historical guests and events vary; this page does not guarantee a particular seat or event.
