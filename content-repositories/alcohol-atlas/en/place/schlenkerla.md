# Schlenkerla

Schlenkerla is a historic beer tavern in Bamberg, with roots going back to 1405. It is known for Rauchbier: the malt is smoked over beechwood, giving the beer a clear campfire, smoke and malt-sweetness character.

## Style

The tavern keeps wooden interiors and a traditional Franconian menu; Rauchbier comes in light and dark versions. The smoke can be intense for a first-time drinker, so start with a small glass.

## Drinking

Pair it with roast pork, sausage and bread. Smoked beer is best not served too cold, or the aroma is suppressed; if the smoke is new to you, begin with the lighter version.

## Boundary

Opening hours, menus and prices are set by official channels. Smoke intensity varies by batch; this page does not rate specific vintages.
