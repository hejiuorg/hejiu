# Harry's Bar Venice

Harry's Bar is near Piazza San Marco in Venice and was founded by Giuseppe Cipriani in 1931. It is known for the Bellini, made with white peach purée and Prosecco, and for carpaccio.

## Style

The room is small, with a compact bar and closely spaced tables; service is formal. The Bellini is the signature aperitif, and carpaccio is usually served as a starter. Prices are high and booking is advisable in season.

## Drinking

Order a Bellini with a simple starter. If you only want the atmosphere, avoid the main meal period; dress sensibly and respect the quiet tone of the room.

## Boundary

Opening hours, bookings and prices are set by Cipriani's official channels. The origins of the Bellini and carpaccio have several versions; this page uses the widely repeated house narrative.
