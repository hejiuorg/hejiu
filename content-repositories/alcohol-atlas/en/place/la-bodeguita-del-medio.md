# La Bodeguita del Medio

La Bodeguita del Medio is in Old Havana and opened in 1942. It is known for Mojitos, Cuban home-style food and the signatures covering its walls; Hemingway's name is often associated with the bar.

## Style

The space is small, with walls full of signatures and photographs and live music. The Mojito is made with rum, lime, mint, sugar and soda; the menu is Cuban home cooking, and the bar is popular with visitors.

## Drinking

Order a Mojito with a simple Cuban dish. Avoid the music peak if you want a quiet conversation; the wall signatures are historic decoration and should not be added to.

## Boundary

Opening hours, menus and prices are set by official channels. Hemingway's relationship with the bar has several versions and is not treated here as the only historical account.
