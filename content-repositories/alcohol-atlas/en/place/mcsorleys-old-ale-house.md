# McSorley's Old Ale House

McSorley's Old Ale House is in the East Village and opened in 1854. It is one of New York's longest-running bars and for many years served only light and dark ale. The interior keeps wooden tables, old posters and gaslight-era fittings.

## Style

The room is small and direct, and orders are often by the glass or round. It long admitted men only; that policy changed in the 1970s. The historic fittings are part of the pub's identity, not an endorsement of past policies.

## Drinking

Order a light or dark ale and take in the old-New York atmosphere. Check current payment rules on site; it is very crowded on weekend nights, so choose a quieter time.

## Boundary

Opening hours, payment methods and prices are set by official channels. Historical policies and famous guests are background, not a reason to recommend the bar.
