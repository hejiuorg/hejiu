# Caffè Florian

Caffè Florian is on Piazza San Marco in Venice and opened in 1720. It is one of Europe's longest-running cafés and a historic meeting place; the interior preserves several ornate rooms.

## Style

The café has several decorated rooms and a terrace facing the square. A small band often plays; prices are well above an ordinary café, and some seats may carry a music surcharge.

## Drinking

Order a coffee or hot chocolate, sit inside to see the rooms, or take a terrace table for the square. Check the seating and music charges before ordering, and avoid midday in high season.

## Boundary

Opening hours, seating charges and performance schedules are set by official channels. Historical guests are background information; not every room is always open.
