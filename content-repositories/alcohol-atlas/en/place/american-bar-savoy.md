# American Bar at The Savoy

The American Bar is inside The Savoy in London and opened with the hotel in 1893. It is known for its classic cocktails and Art Deco interior; Harry Craddock published The Savoy Cocktail Book in 1930.

## Style

The bar continues the American cocktail tradition, with drinks such as the White Lady and Hanky Panky often cited. There is live piano music and the dress code is relatively formal; menus and performances change.

## Drinking

Order a classic cocktail and, if possible, sit at the bar to watch the bartenders work. Ask for a current recommendation; prices are high, so book ahead and check the dress code.

## Boundary

Opening hours, booking rules and dress requirements are set by The Savoy's official channels. This page does not guarantee historical anecdotes or celebrity sightings.
