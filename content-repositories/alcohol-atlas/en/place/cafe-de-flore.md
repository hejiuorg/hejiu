# Café de Flore

Café de Flore is in the 6th arrondissement of Paris, in Saint-Germain-des-Prés, and opened in the late 19th century. In the 20th century it became a meeting place for writers, philosophers and artists, and it remains a symbol of the Parisian literary café.

## Style

The interior keeps red seats, mirrors and a traditional café layout, while the terrace is good for watching the street. Hot chocolate, coffee and French breakfast are staples; prices are high and service is unhurried.

## Drinking

Come in the morning or afternoon for coffee and people-watching. Avoid weekend afternoons if you want a quieter conversation, and check whether a terrace service charge applies before ordering.

## Boundary

Opening hours, menus and prices are set by official channels. Literary guests are history, not a promise of a particular seat or encounter.
