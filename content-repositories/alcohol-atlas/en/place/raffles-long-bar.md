# Raffles Long Bar

The Long Bar is inside Raffles Hotel Singapore, which opened in 1887. It is known for the Singapore Sling, a long drink of gin, cherry brandy, citrus and pineapple juice usually credited to bartender Ngiam Tong Boon in the early 20th century.

## Style

The bar keeps a colonial-style veranda, rattan chairs and ceiling fans, with a relaxed atmosphere. The Singapore Sling is sweet and relatively low in alcohol; peanuts are served, and the traditional permission to drop the shells on the floor is part of the ritual.

## Drinking

Order a Singapore Sling with peanuts and snacks. Prices are at hotel-bar level; book ahead or avoid the early evening in high season.

## Boundary

Opening hours, bookings and prices are set by Raffles Hotel's official channels. The creation date and original recipe of the Singapore Sling have several versions; this page uses the widely cited hotel narrative.
