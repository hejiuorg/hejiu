# U Fleků

U Fleků is in Prague's New Town and was founded in 1499. It is one of the longest-running beer halls and has its own microbrewery, known for dark lager and Czech home-style food.

## Style

The building has several historic halls and a garden, with accordion music. Staff carry trays of beer between tables; the dark lager has a clear malt aroma and moderate bitterness. It is popular with visitors and prices are at Prague tourist levels.

## Drinking

Order a dark lager with roast duck, pork or bread dumplings. If you only want a small taste, ask for a smaller glass; live music may involve a tip.

## Boundary

Opening hours, menus and prices are set by official channels. The founding year follows the brewery's public history; check the on-site label for the beer's strength.
