# Gujing Wine Culture Museum

The Gujing Wine Culture Museum is in Gujing Town, Qiaocheng District, Bozhou, Anhui, opposite the Gujing distillery. Public information from the Bozhou municipal government says the museum was built by Gujing Group in 1994 and opened in 1996. In 2008, the wider Gujing Wine Culture Park was rated a national 4A tourist attraction.
