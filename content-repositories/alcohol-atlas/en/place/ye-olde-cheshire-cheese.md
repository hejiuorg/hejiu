# Ye Olde Cheshire Cheese

Ye Olde Cheshire Cheese is on Fleet Street in London; the current building was rebuilt after the Great Fire of 1667. It keeps narrow stairs, wood-panelled rooms and a cellar, and is one of London's historic pubs.

## Style

The pub has several low-ceilinged rooms and fireplaces, serving British beer, pies and pub food. It is dark and cramped, and busy after work; there is no particular dress code.

## Drinking

Order a British ale with a pie or fish and chips. For a quieter conversation, avoid the after-work rush; the cellar seats have the strongest sense of history.

## Boundary

Opening hours, menus and prices are set by official channels. The site's history goes back further, but the current building is dated to the 1667 reconstruction; check the official history for detail.
