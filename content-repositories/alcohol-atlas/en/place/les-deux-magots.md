# Les Deux Magots

Les Deux Magots is on Place Saint-Germain-des-Prés in Paris and opened in 1885. The name comes from two statues of Chinese merchants inside; in the 20th century it became a meeting place for writers and intellectuals, including Hemingway, Sartre and Beauvoir.

## Style

The interior has mirrors, wooden seats and a terrace, and is somewhat quieter than Café de Flore. Coffee, hot chocolate and French pastries are staples, with light meals also available.

## Drinking

Come for morning coffee or an afternoon sit. The terrace faces the square and is good for people-watching; inside is better for conversation. Prices reflect the Saint-Germain tourist area.

## Boundary

Opening hours, menus and prices are set by official channels. Famous guests are historical context, not a guarantee of current quality or a particular seat.
