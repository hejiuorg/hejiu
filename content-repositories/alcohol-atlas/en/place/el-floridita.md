# El Floridita

El Floridita is in Old Havana and was founded in 1817. It is one of Cuba's best-known bars and became famous in the 20th century for Hemingway's visits, frozen Daiquiris and its seafood bar.

## Style

The bar keeps a long counter, mirrors and red tones, with live music. The frozen Daiquiri is cold, sweet and tart; the seafood bar serves Cuban-style seafood. It is popular with visitors and prices are at tourist levels.

## Drinking

Order a frozen Daiquiri and watch the room from the bar or a corner. If you prefer less sweetness, ask the bartender to reduce the syrup; arrive early at meal times.

## Boundary

Opening hours, menus and prices are set by official channels. Hemingway's visits are historical, but a “Hemingway recipe” is brand storytelling, not the only authentic version.
