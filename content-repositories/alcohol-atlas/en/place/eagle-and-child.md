# The Eagle and Child

The Eagle and Child is on St Giles' in Oxford, in a building whose history goes back to the 17th century. In the 20th century, Tolkien, C.S. Lewis and other Inklings met here, making it a stop on Oxford's literary map.

## Style

The pub has several rooms and a courtyard, serving British beer, pies and pub food. It is part of a pub group, so menus and opening arrangements can change; the historic literary room is not always open.

## Drinking

Order a British beer with a pie or Sunday roast if available. Ask staff which rooms are connected with the Inklings; treat it as a working pub, not a museum.

## Boundary

Opening hours, menus and room access are set by official channels. The Inklings meetings are historical fact, but the exact seats and drinks cannot be reconstructed.
