# Hofbräuhaus

Hofbräuhaus is in central Munich and was founded in 1589 as the Bavarian court brewery. It opened to the public in the 19th century and became a symbol of Munich beer-hall culture.

## Style

The halls have long tables, copper barrels and live band music, with a lively atmosphere. It serves its own beer and Bavarian dishes such as roast pork, white sausage and pretzels; sharing tables is common.

## Drinking

Order a litre of beer with roast pork or white sausage. If a large glass is too much, order a smaller size; service is busy at peak times, so avoid weekend evenings.

## Boundary

Opening hours, menus and prices are set by official channels. It is a tourist-heavy venue and groups may be present; this page does not guarantee seating or performances.
