# Café Central

Café Central is in central Vienna and opened in 1876. It was a meeting place for intellectuals and writers including Trotsky and Zweig, and is one of the symbols of Viennese coffee-house culture.

## Style

The café has vaulted ceilings, stone columns and piano music, with a classical atmosphere. It serves Viennese coffee, apple strudel and cakes, as well as Austrian dishes at meal times. It is popular with visitors and queues are common in high season.

## Drinking

Order a Wiener Melange and a slice of cake, and take your time. For a quieter read, avoid the afternoon and dinner peaks; piano music adds atmosphere but also volume.

## Boundary

Opening hours, menus and prices are set by the café's official channels. Historical guests are background, not a guarantee of a particular seat or performance.
