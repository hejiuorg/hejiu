# Negroni recipe (IBA version)

This is a sourced version of the [Negroni](ref:cocktail). Recipe versions are kept separate from the cocktail identity so that other sourced versions can sit alongside without overwriting this one.

## Recipe

- 30 ml gin
- 30 ml Campari
- 30 ml sweet vermouth
- Ice, as needed
- 1 piece orange peel

## Method

1. Add ice to a mixing glass.
2. Pour in the gin, Campari and sweet vermouth; stir until the glass is well chilled.
3. Place a large ice cube in a [rocks glass](ref:vessel) and strain the drink into it.
4. Express the orange peel over the drink and place it as a garnish.

## Units and substitutions

This page uses millilitres and does not automatically convert to ounces or “parts”. Changing the gin, bitter aperitif or vermouth creates another drink; the same recipe title should not be used without stating the difference.
