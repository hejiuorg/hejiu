# Rocks glass

A rocks glass (also called an old-fashioned glass or lowball) is a short, wide-mouthed glass tumbler. It is used for spirits served over ice and for cocktails that are strained or built over ice.

## Shape and use

- The short, wide bowl leaves room for a large ice cube.
- The wide mouth releases aroma and gives space for a garnish such as orange peel.
- There is no stem, so the hand warms the glass; hold it near the rim or use a coaster when you want to keep the drink cold longer.
- Capacities vary. Adjust ice to your own glass rather than assuming every rocks glass holds the same volume.

## A real example

The IBA Negroni recipe strains the stirred drink into a rocks glass over ice and garnishes it with orange peel. See the [Negroni recipe](ref:recipe).
